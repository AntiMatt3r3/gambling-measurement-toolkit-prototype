Measurement option · M0060

GDIT

Gambling Disorder Identification Test

Instrument

Fourteen-item treatment-outcome-oriented instrument covering gambling behaviour, symptoms and negative consequences.

Gambling-related harmGambling severity / disorderGambling behaviour and expenditure
Development-stage form

This record is the 14-item development form. Responsiveness, meaningful change and a validated cut-off were not established in the curated development source.

Object type
Instrument
Intended use
Baseline / needs assessment · Clinical / treatment monitoring · Intervention / outcome evaluation · Research / mechanism testing
Data source / respondent
Participant self-report — Person who gambles
Population / setting
People who gamble, including treatment-seeking people with gambling disorder — Treatment and research
Time basis
Past 12 months; appendix uses past month
Gambling specificity
Gambling-specific

Instrument specification

Using the instrument

Version / form
Development-stage 14-item GDIT form
Items / criteria
14 — Fourteen core items plus appendix items described in the development paper.
Timeframe
Past 12 months for core symptom/behaviour items; appendix expenditure items use past month
Administration
Self-report
Response format
Item-specific frequency categories; development form also includes an appendix
Burden / feasibility
No authoritative fixed completion time established

Scoring and interpretation

Three content domains: gambling behaviour, gambling symptoms and negative consequences. The development paper did not establish a validated total-score threshold; do not use an inferred cut-off.

Range or output: No validated total range/cut-off established in development paper

Direction: Higher-frequency responses generally indicate more frequent/problematic features; no validated global interpretation threshold established.

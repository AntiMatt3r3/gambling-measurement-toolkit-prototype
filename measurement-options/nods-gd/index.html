Scoring and interpretation

One point per DSM-5 criterion. Published DSM-5 severity categories are mild 4–5, moderate 6–7 and severe 8–9; scores below 4 do not meet the DSM-5 count threshold.

Range or output: 0–9 criteria

Direction: Higher criterion counts indicate greater DSM-5 gambling-disorder severity.

DSM-5-adapted screening/assessment form; original validation sample was Canadian online participants and is not the sole intended population.

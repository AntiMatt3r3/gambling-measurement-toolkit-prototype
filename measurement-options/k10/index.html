Scoring and interpretation

Sum items to 10–50 using Australian scoring. Higher scores indicate greater distress. Category boundaries vary by jurisdiction and purpose; record the chosen convention.

Range or output: 10–50 under Australian scoring; 0–40 under some US conventions

Direction: Higher scores indicate greater non-specific psychological distress.

K10 total

Raw total

Range/output: 10–50 Australian scoring

Derivation: Sum ten 1–5 ratings.

Category conventions vary; retain scoring jurisdiction.

Scoring and interpretation

Sum items. Authors describe mild 8–20, moderate 21–30, severe 31–40 and extreme 41–48; symptom categories, not diagnoses.

Range or output: 0–48

Direction: Higher scores indicate greater gambling-symptom severity.

Primary score

Raw symptom total with published severity bands

Range/output: 0–48

Derivation: Sum items. Authors describe mild 8–20, moderate 21–30, severe 31–40 and extreme 41–48; symptom categories, not diagnoses.

Designed as a gambling-symptom severity/change measure; not a population diagnostic instrument.

Evaluation use

Measurement of change

Limited / not established in this curation

Primary validation includes change-monitoring intent and treatment-course change; no independently established meaningful-change threshold was verified in A1.

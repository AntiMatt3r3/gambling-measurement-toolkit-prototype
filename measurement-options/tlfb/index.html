Structured method

How the method works

Calendar-based retrospective reconstruction of gambling behaviour across a specified period.

Timing
Baseline and repeated follow-up as specified.
Anchoring / prompts
Calendar and personally salient event anchors support day/event recall.
Recording granularity
Day- or event-level before aggregation.
Administration
Interview or structured self-completion.
Outputs
Gambling type, frequency, duration, intent, risk, win/loss and alcohol use while gambling; implementation must define selected outputs.
Configurable parameters
Recall window, gambling-event definition and selected output fields.
Data-quality considerations: Recall bias, incomplete expenditure recall and inconsistent definitions of episodes/win-loss.

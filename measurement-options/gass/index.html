Scoring and interpretation

Rate confidence to abstain in 21 high-risk situations. The developer provides a scoring key for total/subscale interpretation; higher scores indicate greater confidence.

Range or output: Total 0–105 if summed; subscale scores depend on the official scoring key

Direction: Higher scores indicate greater confidence in abstaining from gambling.

Primary score

Total and four situational subscales

Range/output: Total 0–105 if summed; subscale scores depend on the official scoring key

Derivation: Rate confidence to abstain in 21 high-risk situations. The developer provides a scoring key for total/subscale interpretation; higher scores indicate greater confidence.

Situational abstinence self-efficacy measure; not a diagnostic screen.

Scoring and interpretation

Calculate mean scores for Family, Friends, Significant Other and optionally all 12 items. Higher scores indicate greater perceived support. Threshold bands are not universal.

Range or output: 1–7 for subscale and total means

Direction: Higher scores indicate greater perceived social support.

Family support

Subscale mean

Range/output: 1–7

Derivation: Mean four Family items.

Friends support

Subscale mean

Range/output: 1–7

Derivation: Mean four Friends items.

Significant Other support

Subscale mean

Range/output: 1–7

Derivation: Mean four Significant Other items.

Overall perceived support

Total mean

Range/output: 1–7

Derivation: Mean all twelve items.

Threshold bands are not universal.

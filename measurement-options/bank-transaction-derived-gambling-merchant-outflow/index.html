Operational definition

How the indicator is derived

Sum of debit outflows classified to gambling merchants across available linked bank accounts in the prior 90 days.

Aggregation rule
Sum qualifying debit transaction amounts after documented merchant classification and exclusions.

Gambling merchant outflow

Sum debit amounts for transactions meeting the documented gambling-merchant classifier.

Aggregation: Person-window · Missing data: Incomplete account/merchant coverage must be reported; do not net against unidentified winnings.

Coverage and missingness: Misses cash, other accounts and unclassified merchants; includes deposits/outflows rather than net loss and may not capture winnings.
Governance: Requires lawful data access, explicit consent/legal basis, minimisation, security, classification governance and disclosure-control review.

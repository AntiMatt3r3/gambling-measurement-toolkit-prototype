Operational definition

How the indicator is derived

Episode classified as planned completion when the service documents completion of the prespecified care plan or agreed treatment goals at episode close.

Aggregation rule
1 for documented planned completion; 0 for other closed episodes; open episodes remain not applicable.

Planned completion status

1 when closed episode has documented planned completion; 0 for other closed episodes.

Aggregation: Person-episode · Missing data: Open episodes are not applicable; unknown exit reason remains missing.

Coverage and missingness: Local discharge coding and care-plan definitions may differ.
Governance: Routine service data; do not infer clinical improvement from completion alone.

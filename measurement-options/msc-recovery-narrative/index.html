Measurement option · MO0009

MSC recovery narrative

Most Significant Change recovery narrative protocol

Qualitative method

Periodic collection and structured review of participant narratives about the most significant recovery-related change.

Motivation, self-efficacy and recoveryWellbeing, quality of life and capability
Object type
Qualitative method
Intended use
Intervention / outcome evaluation · Service / experience evaluation · Process / implementation evaluation · Research / mechanism testing
Data source / respondent
Participant self-report — Other participant / service user
Population / setting
Participants in recovery/support programmes — Service, community or research
Time basis
Defined review/follow-up points
Gambling specificity
Adapted for gambling

Qualitative approach

Collection and interpretation

Prototype gambling-recovery application of the Most Significant Change technique. Participants provide a change narrative; a defined review process selects and documents significant stories and reasons.

Respondent / sample
Participants in recovery/support programmes; sampling must be documented.
Prompt / topic structure
Defined change-domain prompt, follow-up probes and reason-for-significance question.
Timing
At planned review/follow-up points.
Collection mode
Interview, written narrative or facilitated group discussion.
Analysis approach
MSC story selection and documented deliberative review; optional thematic analysis must be separately specified.
Interpretive output
Contextualised narratives of important change and programme learning.
Feasibility and quality considerations: Selection is deliberative and context dependent; not representative prevalence estimation.

Scoring and interpretation

Sum or average according to the source protocol; five recognised subscales are gambling expectancies, illusion of control, predictive control, inability to stop gambling and interpretative bias.

Range or output: Total 23–161; subscale ranges depend on item allocation

Direction: Higher scores indicate stronger gambling-related cognitive distortions.

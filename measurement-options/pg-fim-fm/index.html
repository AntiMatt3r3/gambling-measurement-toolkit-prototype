Scoring and interpretation

Items can be summarised across financial, increased-responsibility and psychosocial impact groupings. A3 does not transfer the published factor/psychometric results from the Problem Gambler form to this Family Member form.

Range or output: 0–42 if all 14 frequency ratings are summed; form-specific validation of this total is not established

Direction: Higher frequencies indicate more reported family impact.

PG-FIM Family Member form

Raw total / content grouping

Range/output: 0–42 if summed

Derivation: Sum fourteen 0–3 frequency ratings; content groupings are financial, responsibility and psychosocial.

Psychometric structure in primary paper applies to Problem Gambler form, not this form.

Scoring and interpretation

Apply the licensed, appropriate value set to the profile; not a simple sum.

Range or output: No universal raw total. Utility index is value-set dependent; UK SF-6Dv2 values may be below 0 and reach 1 for full health.

Direction: Higher utility values represent better health; dimension level direction follows the official form.

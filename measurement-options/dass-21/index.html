Scoring and interpretation

Sum seven items within each subscale. Raw subscale scores are 0–21; multiply by two only when applying full-DASS conventions/categories. Do not treat as a diagnostic instrument.

Range or output: 0–21 per raw subscale; 0–42 after optional ×2 convention

Direction: Higher scores indicate more severe negative emotional symptoms.

Measurement option · MO0003

Net gambling loss in the past 30 days

Total stakes minus total returns over a defined 30-day window, in a stated currency.

Direct indicator

Total stakes minus total returns over a defined 30-day window, in a stated currency.

Operational definition

How the indicator is derived

Total stakes minus total returns across all included gambling in the prior 30 days, expressed as a positive loss or negative net win in a stated currency.

Output / unit
currency
Observation window
30 days
Data source
Participant self-report

Not available in this prototype

Aggregation rule
Sum stakes and returns, then compute stakes minus returns.

Net gambling loss

Total stakes minus total returns in the stated currency.

Aggregation: Person-window · Missing data: Missing gambling activities/amounts remain a coverage limitation.

Coverage and missingness: Recall error and inconsistent inclusion of bonuses, credits or shared play; record currency and coverage.
Governance: Financial data are sensitive; minimise collection and restrict access.

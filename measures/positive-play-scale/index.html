Scoring and interpretation

Calculate the four published subscale means after direction coding. The primary paper does not establish a single overall total score.

Range or output: Four subscale means, each 1–7; no validated single total established

Direction: Higher subscale scores indicate more positive-play beliefs or behaviours.

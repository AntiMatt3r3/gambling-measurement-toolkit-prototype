Scoring and interpretation

Sum responses. Original categories: 0, 1–2, 3–7 and 8+. Screening categories are not diagnoses.

Recorded interpretation rules

  • Non-problem: 0 to 0 — Original published scoring context; not gambling-specific validation for generic tools. (Not a stand-alone diagnosis or a meaningful-change threshold.)
  • Low risk: 1 to 2 — Original published scoring context; not gambling-specific validation for generic tools. (Not a stand-alone diagnosis or a meaningful-change threshold.)
  • Moderate risk: 3 to 7 — Original published scoring context; not gambling-specific validation for generic tools. (Not a stand-alone diagnosis or a meaningful-change threshold.)
  • Problem gambling: 8 to 27 — Original published scoring context; not gambling-specific validation for generic tools. (Not a stand-alone diagnosis or a meaningful-change threshold.)

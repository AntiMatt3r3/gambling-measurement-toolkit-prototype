Scoring and interpretation

Count endorsed harms. Higher scores indicate more reported harm; not a diagnostic screen.

Range or output: 0–10

Direction: Higher counts indicate more endorsed gambling-related harms.

Primary score

Raw endorsement count; optional population weighting is distinct

Range/output: 0–10

Derivation: Count endorsed harms. Higher scores indicate more reported harm; not a diagnostic screen.

Developed to quantify gambling-related harm, not to diagnose gambling disorder.

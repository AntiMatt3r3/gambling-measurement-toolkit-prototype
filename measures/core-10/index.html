Using the instrument

Version / form
CORE-10 v1.1 (2022 instruction-only revision)
Items / criteria
10
Timeframe
Past week
Administration
Self-report, suitable for repeated/session-by-session use
Response format
Ten items with five response options scored 0–4; two positively worded items are reverse scored
Burden / feasibility
Brief; no fixed official completion time located

Scoring and interpretation

Sum when complete. If nine answered, divide sum by nine and multiply by ten. Do not score with more than one omission.

Range or output: 0–40

Direction: Higher scores indicate greater psychological distress.

Primary score

Raw distress total

Range/output: 0–40

Derivation: Sum when complete. If nine answered, divide sum by nine and multiply by ten. Do not score with more than one omission.

Designed for routine/repeated measurement of global psychological distress; not gambling-specific.

Evaluation use

Measurement of change

Some change evidence curated

Designed for repeated monitoring; reliable-change information is available from the primary paper, but reliable change is not a minimal important difference.

An intended use such as “Intervention / outcome evaluation” does not by itself establish responsiveness or a meaningful-change threshold.

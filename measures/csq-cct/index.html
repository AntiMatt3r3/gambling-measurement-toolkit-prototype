Scoring and interpretation

Seven ratings and four open questions were analysed as study-specific service-experience data. No validated composite score or thresholds were identified.

Range or output: Not applicable: item-level ratings plus qualitative responses; no validated total score

Direction: Higher individual ratings indicate greater satisfaction; no total-score direction is established.

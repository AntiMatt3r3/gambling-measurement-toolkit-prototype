About this prototype

Purpose, scope and methods

A transparent first module for navigating gambling-harms outcome measures, built from a structured research database.

Purpose

The toolkit is intended for public-health teams, third-sector organisations, treatment and service providers, commissioners, researchers and evaluators. This proof of concept addresses one immediate question: what measures are available, and which might fit my evaluation needs?

Inclusion in the database is not endorsement. The site does not rank measures, nominate a “best” instrument or reduce psychometric evidence to a global score.

Scope of the current prototype

The public layer contains 24 measure or form records across 9 populated outcome domains. It includes gambling-specific instruments, generic mental-health and wellbeing measures, health-utility or capability measures, affected-other forms and a service-experience measure. Distinct versions remain distinct records.

This is deliberately not an exhaustive repository. It is a representative, partially curated proof of concept derived from a broader source-normalisation database.

How measures entered the database

The canonical workbook preserves records from an original evidence review alongside later targeted curation additions. The website uses its dedicated PUBLIC_PROTOTYPE_EXPORT as the population boundary, then joins existing structured records for domains, versions, evidence findings, use studies, access resources and health-economic relationships.

Original review-derived records and targeted additions retain their provenance. Application or use of a measure in a study is kept distinct from evidence about its measurement properties.

Evidence and curation states

Where available, individual findings retain the publication, sample, measurement property, method/statistic, estimate, uncertainty, population, language or version, timepoint and source locator. Findings are presented in context rather than converted into stars, traffic lights or overall psychometric judgements.

Verified

Checked against the cited source for the recorded claim.

Partially verified

Some important fields have been checked, but curation remains incomplete.

Needs verification

A claim or identity remains unresolved and should not be treated as settled.

Not reviewed

The property has not yet been assessed in this bounded curation. This is not the same as “no evidence”.

Access and licensing

Instrument use and reproduction are treated separately. An article’s publication licence or a website footer is not taken as an instrument licence. Where rights or permissions remain uncertain, the site preserves that uncertainty and directs users to the recorded owner or access route where available.

The wider Evaluation and Monitoring Toolkit

The measurement database is the first functioning component of a wider toolkit intended to connect interventions, evaluation approaches, outcomes, measures and interpretation. Future modules may address programme theory, evaluation design, mixed methods, implementation and process evaluation, templates, outcome selection and economic evaluation.

The wider direction is compatible with the MRC/NIHR framework for developing and evaluating complex interventions, particularly its emphasis on context, programme theory, stakeholder perspectives, uncertainty and iterative evaluation. This prototype does not attempt to reproduce that guidance.

Version

Public data schema: 2.0-public-web. Generated from Gambling_Harms_Measurement_Database_v2_checkpoint.xlsx. The workbook remains the canonical research source; the site data are a read-only derivative for presentation.
